Our Thesis

Acquire Earlier. Build Deliberately. Own for the Long Term.

Momo seeks to identify promising music IP before it reaches mature institutional valuations, then develop the artist, audience and commercial infrastructure around the asset to strengthen long-term value.

Develop stronger artist ecosystems and catalogs positioned for long-term ownership, strategic partnership and future growth.
