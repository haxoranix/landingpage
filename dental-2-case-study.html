Project overview

Warm, modern dentistry without losing clinical credibility.

The concept reframes a dental clinic website around calm communication, approachable colour and a clean editorial structure. The visual system avoids cold medical blue, generic stock-card layouts and overly technical presentation.

ObjectiveCreate a contemporary dental clinic presence that feels reassuring, professional and distinctly local.
AudienceAdults, families and new patients looking for preventive, restorative and cosmetic dental care in Cape Town.
Visual toneSoft, calm, warm, clean, approachable and clinically credible.
Content flowPositioning, treatments, patient experience, local clinic information, FAQ and appointment request.
BuildSingle-page responsive HTML, CSS and JavaScript concept.
Creative direction

A softer healthcare identity for a clinic that should feel easy to walk into.

The design uses muted South African coastal tones, generous white space and natural photography. Rounded surfaces are kept controlled, while the typography stays direct and editorial. The result is friendly without becoming playful, and premium without feeling exclusive.

Colour system

Soft colour with enough contrast to stay professional.

Warm cream creates the base. Sage carries the core identity, while peach, powder blue and lavender add variation across supporting sections. Deep green anchors buttons, headings and high-contrast moments.

Typography

A modern grotesk system with natural spacing.

Manrope handles the main display and heading hierarchy. DM Sans carries navigation, labels, body copy and interface text. The pairing keeps the website contemporary while staying readable and practical for a healthcare setting.
