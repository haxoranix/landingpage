Frequently asked questions

Everything You Need to Know Before You Start

Clear answers about cards, security, transfers and how HexaPay keeps your finances organized.

Still have a question? ↗
What is HexaPay and how does it work?+

HexaPay combines digital wallets, payment cards, transfers and expense tracking in one dashboard. You can create wallets for different goals, fund them, spend from your card and review activity in real time.

Is my money and personal data secure?+

HexaPay is designed around layered account security, encrypted data handling, fraud monitoring and secure authentication. The interface also gives you quick access to card controls and account activity.

Do I get a physical card with my account?+

Yes. Eligible accounts can use a physical card that stays connected to the same HexaPay balance and transaction history as the digital wallet experience.

Can I create multiple wallets?+

Yes. Create separate wallets for bills, savings, travel, emergencies or any other goal, then move funds between them whenever your priorities change.

How quickly are transfers processed?+

Transfers inside HexaPay are designed to feel immediate. External transfer timing can vary by payment rail, receiving institution and region, and the app shows status throughout the process.

Can I upgrade or change my plan later?+

Yes. You can move between available plans as your transfer, withdrawal and spending needs evolve. Your wallets and transaction history remain in place when you change plans.
